The printer converts readable patterns between flavors with warnings (US-07), and translates PCRE/Python/Java/.NET exactly into ECMAScript for execution: atomic groups, possessive quantifiers, \A/\z/\Z, PCRE `$`, Unicode classes, inline flags.
